## Master Data Management

Navigation: Manage Expense Category, Manage Payment Method and Manage Expense are grouped under the Employees menu folder. Manage Trip is additionally surfaced under Employees → Trip Management. These are the existing screens surfaced in a new location; their data points, business rules and business actions are unchanged.

### Manage Expense Category
- Description: Maintain the standard list of expense categories (e.g., Travel, Lodging, Meals, Transport, Miscellaneous) used to classify every expense for approval routing and analytics.
- Data points: CATEGORY_CODE, CATEGORY_NAME, DESCRIPTION, STATUS (Active/Inactive), CREATED_BY, CREATED_DATE.
- Business rules: CATEGORY_CODE is unique and mandatory; CATEGORY_NAME is mandatory.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit. (Delete is not available — category records cannot be deleted from this screen; use STATUS = Inactive to retire a category.)
- Additional data management: Inactive categories are hidden from the Category picker on Manage Expense but remain visible on historical records and Visuals.

### Manage Payment Method
- Description: Maintain the list of payment methods employees can select when recording how an expense was paid (e.g., Cash, Corporate Card, Personal Card, Bank Transfer).
- Data points: PAYMENT_METHOD_CODE, PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME, TYPE (Cash/Card/Bank Transfer), DESCRIPTION, STATUS (Active/Inactive), CREATED_BY, CREATED_DATE, CATEGORY_CODE (mandatory, linked to Manage Expense Category), CATEGORY_NAME (mandatory, auto-filled from the linked Expense Category).
- Business rules: PAYMENT_METHOD_CODE is unique and mandatory; PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME is mandatory; CATEGORY_CODE is mandatory and must exist in the Manage Expense Category master; CATEGORY_NAME is mandatory and is auto-populated from the selected Expense Category.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit. (Delete is not available — payment method records cannot be deleted from this screen; use STATUS = Inactive to retire a payment method.)
- Additional data management: Inactive payment methods are hidden from the picker on Manage Expense but remain visible on historical records and Visuals.

### Module independence
- The Employee screens and the Management screens are independently manageable — changes to one do not affect the other.
- This change does not alter Master Data Management, Expense, Trip or Approval behaviour.

## Approval Management

### Approval Rules
- Description: Configure approval routing rules — which approver role handles a report based on Expense Category and/or Total Amount range.
- Data points: RULE_ID (system auto-generated), CATEGORY (optional lookup to Manage Expense Category, blank = applies to all categories), MIN_AMOUNT, MAX_AMOUNT, APPROVER_ROLE (Manager), STATUS (Active/Inactive), CREATED_BY, CREATED_DATE.
- Business rules: APPROVER_ROLE is mandatory; if both MIN_AMOUNT and MAX_AMOUNT are set, MAX_AMOUNT must be greater than or equal to MIN_AMOUNT.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit, Delete.

## Trip Management

### Manage Trip
- Description: Record a business trip that groups related expenses (e.g., a client visit or business travel) so expenses can be tagged and totalled by trip.
- Data points: TRIP_NAME, PURPOSE, DESTINATION, START_DATE, END_DATE, EMPLOYEE, STATUS (dropdown: Planned/Upcoming/Completed, default Planned; Closed is system-set only), TOTAL_EXPENSE_AMOUNT, CREATED_DATE.
- Business rules: TRIP_NAME, DESTINATION, START_DATE and END_DATE are mandatory; END_DATE must be on or after START_DATE; trip-expense linking has been removed and TOTAL_EXPENSE_AMOUNT is no longer auto-calculated; Serial Number is entered manually by the user (not auto-generated). STATUS is presented as a user-selectable dropdown with exactly three user-selectable values — Planned, Upcoming and Completed — and defaults to Planned on Add. 'Closed' remains a system-only status, set solely by the Close-trip action, and is never selectable manually (it is not offered as a dropdown option). TRIP_ID is the primary key and is system auto-generated on Add in the format TRIP-00001 (prefix "TRIP-" followed by a zero-padded 5-digit sequence number, e.g. TRIP-00001, TRIP-00002, ...), well within the 20-character limit; it is no longer left to a platform-level fallback ID and is not manually entered.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit, Delete, Close-trip.
- Note: the prior rule blocking selection of a Closed trip on an expense has been removed (validation logic deleted due to a schema-reference defect).
- Additional data management: Closing a trip sets STATUS = Closed; reopening is not permitted in the standard flow.

## Expense Management

### Manage Expense
- Description: Capture an individual expense incurred by the employee, attach supporting documentation, and make it available for inclusion in an expense report.
- Data points: EXPENSE_ID, EXPENSE_DATE, CATEGORY, AMOUNT (Total Expense Amount, numeric/currency, manually entered), CURRENCY (dropdown: INR/USD/EUR/GBP), PAYMENT_METHOD, MERCHANT_NAME, DESCRIPTION, RECEIPT_ATTACHMENT, EMPLOYEE, EXPENSE_REPORT (link, populated once added to a report), STATUS (Draft/Added-to-Report/Submitted/Approved/Rejected), PAYMENT_PROCESS_STATUS (system-maintained, read-only on this screen; allowed values are None, Proceed and Not Proceed; defaults to 'None', indicating the payment has not yet been processed, until it is updated elsewhere in the payment process flow to "Proceed" or "Not Proceed" once the expense's payment is processed from the Payment Process screen).
- Business rules: AMOUNT is the manually-entered Total Expense Amount, mandatory and must be greater than 0; CURRENCY is mandatory, selected from a dropdown with allowed values INR, USD, EUR, GBP (default INR), and indicates the currency the AMOUNT is denominated in; EXPENSE_DATE cannot be a future date; CATEGORY and PAYMENT_METHOD are mandatory and must exist in the respective masters; once its parent Expense Report is Submitted, the expense is locked from edit until the report is rejected or the line is removed; the TRIP field on Expense (linking an expense to its trip) previously matched EXPTRACK_TRIP.TRIP_ID; since TRIP_ID has been removed from Manage Trip, this link is now broken for both new and existing records until a new trip-matching column is established; STATUS is system-maintained: it is automatically set to Draft when a new expense record is created and is displayed read-only on the form — the user cannot select or change it from a dropdown; subsequent status changes (Submitted/Approved/Rejected) are driven by the submit and approval actions; STATUS defaults to Draft on every newly created expense and is system-controlled — it is displayed read-only (no dropdown, not user-editable) and is changed only by the Submit action and the approval flow.
- STATUS is NOT shown or entered on the Manage Expense screen — it is set by the system to Pending when the expense is created and is thereafter maintained only by the Approve Expense Report screen, where STATUS is required for the approve/reject decision.
- Note: This Status behaviour applies only to the Manage Expense screen.
- Business rules: Submit saves the expense and, in the same commit, creates a new EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT header for the logged-in employee (REPORT_ID system-generated, EMPLOYEE = logged-in user, STATUS = Submitted) or reuses the employee's existing open (Draft/Submitted) report when one already exists; the submitted expense is linked to it via EXPTRACK_EXPENSE.EXPENSE_REPORT = EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.REPORT_ID and its EXPTRACK_EXPENSE.STATUS is set to Pending. Because Approve Expense Report fetches only EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT rows with STATUS = Submitted, the submitted expense appears in Approval Management automatically with no manual hand-off. An expense already linked to a Submitted/Approved report cannot be submitted again.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit, Delete, Submit.
- Additional data management: adding/removing an expense from a report recalculates the report's TOTAL_AMOUNT; when the parent report is approved or rejected, EXPENSE.STATUS is updated to mirror the report outcome.
- Submit validates and saves the expense, sets STATUS=Submitted and APPROVAL_STATUS=Pending, and automatically creates a one-expense Expense Report (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT, STATUS=Submitted, PAYMENT_STATUS=Pending) linked via EXPENSE_REPORT so it appears in Approval Management (exptrack_approve_expense_report) for approval; the Expense ID is preserved.
- Expense approval workflow: when the employee clicks Submit on Manage Expense, the expense record's STATUS is set to Submitted and APPROVAL_STATUS is set to Pending, with CHG_DATE stamped to the current date/time; the submitted expense then appears in Approve Expense Report for approver action.
- Status-transition rule (Submit): on Submit, the current expense record is updated in place — STATUS is set to Submitted and APPROVAL_STATUS is set to Pending (CHG_DATE is stamped with the update time). The same Expense ID is linked to (an existing Draft, or a newly created) EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT record for the employee, which becomes visible/actionable on Approve Expense Report.
- On Submit, the expense record is validated and saved, STATUS is set to Submitted and APPROVAL_STATUS is set to Pending. The submitted expense is automatically made available for approval in Approval Management (exptrack_approve_expense_report) by creating (or, if one already exists for this expense, reusing) a linked EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT record referencing the same EXPENSE_ID via the expense's EXPENSE_REPORT column, so no duplicate approval record is ever created for the same expense.
- Note: the prior rule blocking edits to a non-editable expense has been removed (validation logic deleted due to a schema-reference defect).
- Clicking Submit on an expense sets its STATUS to Submitted and automatically creates a new Expense Report (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT) containing only this expense, sets that report's STATUS to Submitted and REPORT_TITLE to a generated value referencing the expense, links the expense to the report via its EXPENSE_REPORT column, and the resulting Submitted report immediately becomes actionable in Approval Management (exptrack_approve_expense_report).
- Submit — available only when the expense STATUS is Draft. Clicking Submit automatically creates a new Expense Report (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT) containing just this one expense (REPORT_TITLE derived from the expense's category and date, EMPLOYEE = the expense's EMPLOYEE, TOTAL_AMOUNT = the expense AMOUNT), sets that report's STATUS to Submitted, and links this expense to the new report (EXPENSE_REPORT = new REPORT_ID, expense STATUS = Submitted). The resulting Submitted report then appears in Approval Management (exptrack_approve_expense_report) for the approver to act on.
- On Submit, if the expense is not yet linked to a report (EXPENSE_REPORT is blank), the system auto-generates a new Report ID and creates a linked record in Approval Management (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT) carrying Employee, Report Date (today), Trip, Total Amount (= expense Amount), Category Name, Payment Method Name, Currency, Receipt Attachment and Description from the expense, sets the new record's Status to Submitted, and writes the generated Report ID back onto the source expense's Expense Report field.

### Expense Report
- Description: exptrack_expense_report_new is the screen where an employee views and edits/corrects an expense voucher that was rejected via the Management → Manage Approval transaction. When the approver sets STATUS = Reject on the Manage Approval record and clicks Submit, the rejected record is returned here for the employee to review, correct and resubmit.
- Data points: EXPENSE_ID (reference to the original expense, read-only), EMPLOYEE (read-only), TRIP (lookup to Manage Trip master, editable), TRIP_NAME (auto-filled from TRIP, read-only), CATEGORY (lookup to Manage Expense Category master, editable), EXPENSE_DATE (editable), PAYMENT_METHOD (lookup to Manage Payment Method master, editable), APPROVER_NAME (read-only, name of the approver who rejected it), RECEIPT_ATTACHMENT (file upload, editable so the employee can replace the receipt), TOTAL_AMOUNT (read-only, auto-filled from the original expense report's total when the voucher is returned for correction).
- Business rules: records only arrive on this screen via the Manage Approval reject flow — none are created manually here.
- Business rules: Delete permanently removes the expense report record from this screen.
- Business actions: Search, Edit, Resubmit, Delete. (Add is not available — this screen never originates new records.)
- Note: this is a separate, independent screen from the existing exptrack_expense_report and Management → Manage Approval sections; this addition does not modify those screens or their data points, business rules or business actions.

## Expense Report Management

### Manage Expense
- Description: Capture an individual expense record with category, trip, payment method and receipt.
- Data points: EXPENSE_DATE, CATEGORY, CATEGORY_NAME (auto-filled), TRIP, TRIP_NAME (auto-filled), TOTAL_EXPENSE_AMOUNT, CURRENCY, PAYMENT_METHOD, PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME (auto-filled), RECEIPT_ATTACHMENT, EXPENSE_REPORT (optional reference), S_NO (manual), EXPENSE_ID (system auto-generated, read-only), ADD_DATE/ADD_USER (auto on create), CHG_DATE/CHG_USER (auto on update).
- Business rules: EXPENSE_DATE, CATEGORY, TRIP, TOTAL_EXPENSE_AMOUNT, CURRENCY and PAYMENT_METHOD are mandatory; CATEGORY must exist in the Manage Expense Category master and auto-fills CATEGORY_NAME; TRIP must exist in the Manage Trip master and auto-fills TRIP_NAME; PAYMENT_METHOD must exist in the Manage Payment Method master and auto-fills PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME; EXPENSE_REPORT is optional and stored as a plain reference (no master validation, since the prior Manage Expense Report object has been removed); EXPENSE_ID is system-generated and unique. Once Submitted (or Approved), the expense record is read-only and cannot be edited; it becomes editable again only if the Manager rejects it (STATUS reverts to Rejected, which remains editable), or while still in Draft. APPROVAL_STATUS is a field on the Expense record itself (no separate approval record); it starts blank on Draft and is automatically set to Pending by the system whenever the user clicks Submit on the expense. STATUS is no longer shown or entered on Manage Expense — it is set by the system to `Pending` when an expense is created and is thereafter maintained only by the approval and report screens.
- Clicking Submit on an expense: (1) sets the expense's STATUS to Submitted; (2) if the expense is not yet linked to a Draft Expense Report for the same EMPLOYEE, automatically creates one (REPORT_ID auto-generated, EMPLOYEE and TRIP carried over from the expense, STATUS=Draft) and links the expense to it via EXPENSE_REPORT, otherwise reuses the existing Draft report for that employee; (3) recalculates the report's TOTAL_AMOUNT as the sum of AMOUNT across all expenses linked to it; (4) sets the linked Expense Report's STATUS to Submitted so it becomes actionable by the manager on the existing Approve Expense Report screen. No separate approval record type is introduced — this reuses the existing Expense Report / Approve Expense Report flow. Category, Currency and Payment Method remain on the expense line and stay visible via the report's linked expense lines; they are not duplicated onto the Expense Report record.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit, Delete, Submit — sets STATUS to Submitted, locks the record from further editing, saves it automatically, and notifies the employee's Manager for approval.
- Submit field mapping: when an employee clicks Submit on Manage Expense, the system auto-creates a linked record on EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT (the approval record) with the following mapping — REPORT_ID = system auto-generated (max 20 characters); EMPLOYEE = the expense's EMPLOYEE; REPORT_DATE = today's date; REPORT_TITLE = the linked Trip's TRIP_NAME; TOTAL_AMOUNT = the expense's AMOUNT; Category (CATEGORY_NAME) = the expense's linked Expense Category's CATEGORY_NAME; Payment Method (PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME) = the expense's linked Payment Method's PAYMENT_METHOD_NAME; CURRENCY = the expense's CURRENCY; RECEIPT_ATTACHMENT = the expense's uploaded receipt; DESCRIPTION = the expense's DESCRIPTION; STATUS = 'Submitted'. All of these fields must display fully (not hidden/truncated) on the Approve Expense Report screen so the approver can review complete expense details before deciding.

## Approval Management

Note: approval decisions are handled entirely by the remaining approval screens (Approve Expense Report / Approval Expense). No expense-report data is removed — all EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT records and their data points remain intact and continue to be used by Expense Report, Approval Expense, Manage Reports and the manager/admin dashboards.

### Approve Expense Report
- Description: The single designated approver reviews a submitted expense report, examines the underlying expense lines and receipts, and records an Approve or Reject decision.
- When an expense is submitted in Manage Expense (exptrack_expense), the same expense record is automatically shown in Manage Approval (exptrack_manage_approval) for approval via a mandatory EXPENSE_ID foreign-key relation to EXPTRACK_EXPENSE.EXPENSE_ID. The Manage Approval record displays: EXPENSE_ID (FK, mandatory), EMPLOYEE, TRIP, CATEGORY, EXPENSE_DATE, Total Expense Amount (AMOUNT), PAYMENT_METHOD, STATUS, and APPROVER (approver name) — all mandatory and auto-populated from the linked expense record.
- Data points: REPORT_ID, EMPLOYEE, REPORT_DATE, TOTAL_AMOUNT, EXPENSE_LINES (read-only: CATEGORY, EXPENSE_DATE, AMOUNT, DESCRIPTION, RECEIPT_ATTACHMENT), STATUS, APPROVER_COMMENTS, DECISION_DATE.
- STATUS is the single approval decision field on this screen: it is presented as a drop-down offering exactly three options — Pending, Approved and Rejected — and defaults to Pending. APPROVER_COMMENTS remain mandatory when STATUS = Rejected.
- Business rules: only reports with STATUS = Submitted are actionable; APPROVER_COMMENTS is mandatory when the decision is Reject; the approver cannot act on a report they own; once decided, the report is locked (Approved/Rejected) and only a resubmission after rejection re-opens it.
- Business rules: On Submit the expense is linked to an EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT (an existing Draft/Submitted report for the same EMPLOYEE is reused, otherwise a new one is created) and the report TOTAL_AMOUNT is recalculated as the sum of AMOUNT over all EXPTRACK_EXPENSE rows carrying that REPORT_ID.
- Business rules: When the decision is Approved, the linked report is set to STATUS = Approved, APPROVAL_DATE = today, APPROVER (label 'Approver Name') = the logged-in approver's NAME captured from their display name (not the short user id), stored read-only, and PAYMENT_STATUS = Pending. When the decision is Rejected the report is set to STATUS = Rejected.
- Business rules: Submit is never blocked by a status validation — an expense that is already Submitted/Approved simply shows an informational prompt and the submit proceeds.
- Business actions: Search, Approve, Reject, View-receipt.
- Business actions: Submit — the approver records the decision in STATUS (Approved/Rejected) and clicks Submit to finalise it.
- Business rules (Submit — approval decision): Submit finalises the approval decision recorded in STATUS (Approved or Rejected). On Submit the linked expense report (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT) is stamped with STATUS = Approved or Rejected, APPROVER (label 'Approver Name') = the logged-in approver's NAME, captured from their display name rather than the short user id and shown read-only, and APPROVAL_DATE = current date/time; APPROVER_COMMENTS is mandatory when the decision is Rejected. When the decision is Approved, PAYMENT_STATUS on the expense report is set to Pending. On a Rejected decision PAYMENT_STATUS is left untouched. Submit is only allowed on records whose decision has been recorded and which have not already been decided.
- Note: each 1:1 approval record (one expense to one Expense Report) additionally carries Trip Name (display, from the Trip master) alongside the already-documented Category Name, Payment Method Name, Currency, Receipt Attachment and Description columns; Approve/Reject (with mandatory rejection comments, auto-stamped Approver and Decision Date) act on these records once their Status is Submitted.
- Additional data management: Approve sets EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS = Approved, propagates STATUS = Approved to all linked Expenses, and stamps APPROVAL_DATE; Reject sets EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS = Rejected and propagates STATUS = Rejected to all linked Expenses.
- When the approver clicks Approve, every expense linked to the report has STATUS set to Approved; when the approver clicks Reject, every expense linked to the report has STATUS set to Rejected. In both cases CHG_DATE is stamped to the current date/time.
- Status-driven routing: The approver's decision writes EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS. When STATUS is set to Rejected the report is automatically returned to Expense Management → Manage Reports where the originating employee can view and correct it. This routing is driven solely by the EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS column; no other field, table or screen participates.
- Approval routing: when the approver sets the Approval Status to Approved, the decision sets EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS = Approved. No manual hand-off is required.
- When the approver sets the Approval Status to Rejected, the decision sets EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS = Rejected, returning the report to the raising employee on Manage Reports (Expense Management), where they can view the approver comments and correct and resubmit it.
- This routing is driven solely by the Approval Status field on Approval Management. No other field, screen or module behaviour changes: Manage Reports keeps its existing criteria, columns and actions.
- Business rules (Approval Status routing): Approval Status routing — when the approver sets Approval Status to Approved, EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS is set to Approved, which automatically surfaces the report on Finance Payment Processing (exptrack_finance_payment), which fetches only reports with STATUS = Approved. When the approver sets Approval Status to Rejected, EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS is set to Rejected and the report is released back to the employee on Expense Management → Manage Reports (exptrack_manage_reports), where the owning employee can view it and correct/resubmit it; APPROVER_COMMENTS remains mandatory on Reject. This routing is driven solely by the Approval Status field on Approval Management — no other field, screen or module behaviour changes. When the approver submits an Approve decision, the expense report header is committed with EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT.STATUS = Approved together with the APPROVER and APPROVAL_DATE. Finance Payment Processing fetches only EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT rows with STATUS = Approved, so an approved report appears in the Finance Payment Processing queue automatically on the next Search — there is no manual hand-off, re-entry or intermediate step between the two screens. A Reject decision commits STATUS = Rejected with mandatory APPROVER_COMMENTS and the report is never released to Finance.

### Manage Approval (Management folder view)
- Description: A list, surfaced in the Management navigation folder, showing existing submitted expense records for reference — it does not create, fetch, or import any new/sample records. The screen now also supports Edit: users can edit a record's STATUS field, a required dropdown with options Approved and Reject; the other columns (Expense ID, Employee, Trip, Category, Expense Date, Total Expense Amount, Payment Method, Approver Name) remain read-only.
- Data points: EXPENSE_ID, EMPLOYEE, TRIP (from the linked expense report), CATEGORY, EXPENSE_DATE, TOTAL_EXPENSE_AMOUNT (the linked expense report's total), PAYMENT_METHOD, STATUS (editable dropdown: Approved/Reject, required), APPROVER (approver name) — all fields are mandatory/always populated on the underlying records.
- Business actions: Search, Edit (STATUS only) — this screen displays records already created via Manage Expense / Manage Expense Report; Edit is limited to updating STATUS and does not otherwise add or alter data.
- Clicking Submit on a Manage Approval record creates a corresponding record in the Finance → Payment Process transaction (exptrack_payment_process), carrying over Expense ID, Employee, Trip, Category, Expense Date, Total Expense Amount, Payment Method and Status from the submitted record. No sample, random, or fetched/imported records are created — only the actual submitted record's data is copied, one Payment Process record per Submit.
- Note: this is a new, separate object from the existing exptrack_manage_approval, exptrack_manage_approval_new, exptrack_manage_approval2, and exptrack_manage_approval_new_txn screens, and none of those existing objects or any workflow are modified by this addition.
- Business rules: When the approver sets STATUS = Reject on a voucher, the linked Expense Report (EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT record identified by the expense's EXPENSE_REPORT column) is automatically set to STATUS = Rejected, returning the voucher to the Expense Management → Expense Report transaction where the employee can revise and resubmit it.
- Business rules (Reject → correction round-trip): When an approver on Manage Approval (exptrack_manage_approval / exptrack_manage_approval_v2) sets STATUS = Reject and clicks Submit, the same expense voucher (same EXPENSE_ID) is returned to the Expense Report screen (exptrack_expense_report_new) in the Expense Management folder for employee correction. The employee can view and edit the returned record and resubmit it to Management Approval using the same EXPENSE_ID. The system must prevent the same EXPENSE_ID from being submitted for approval more than once while a submission for it is already pending or approved — resubmission is only permitted for records currently in rejected/correction status. No other workflow (Trip, Payment Method, Budget Limitation, Finance Payment/Processing) is affected by this change.
- Business rules: When the approver sets STATUS = Reject and clicks Submit, the record is also linked to the Expense Report screen (exptrack_expense_report_new / EXPTRACK_EXPENSE_REPORT_NEW): a record for the same EXPENSE_ID is created if it does not already exist, or updated if it does, with every field (Employee, Trip, Category, Expense Date, Payment Method, Total Amount, Approver Name, Receipt Attachment) copied over exactly as on the Manage Approval record — no field is altered.

## Finance

### Budget and Limitation
- Description: Record finance-related budget and limitation details including the finance owner, department, and supporting budget document.
- Data points: FINANCE_NAME, DEPARTMENT, BUDGET_DOCUMENT.
- Business rules: FINANCE_NAME, DEPARTMENT and BUDGET_DOCUMENT are all mandatory.
- Business rules: Users with the Employee role can only View Budget and Limitation records — a View action is available to them and they cannot Edit records. All other roles retain the existing Search, Add, Edit and Delete actions unchanged.
- Business actions: Search, Add, Edit, Delete, View (View available to the Employee role; Employee role cannot Edit). A Download action is available next to the Budget Document field to download the uploaded budget document file.

### Payment Process
- Description: The finance processor reviews an approved expense report and processes its payment, recording the payment outcome against the expense.
- Data points: Expense ID, Employee, Trip, Category, Expense Date, Total Expense Amount, Payment Method, Receipt Attachment, Status, Approver Name (all read-only reference fields carried over from the approved expense record), Finance Name (editable), Payment Process Status (editable dropdown: Proceed/Not Proceed).
- Business rules: Payment Process Status is an editable dropdown field, offering exactly two options — Proceed and Not Proceed — that the finance processor sets before processing the payment.
- Business actions: Search, Process Payment.
